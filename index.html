01 · project brief

the brief.

01

skye in her zone, up in the trees.

a delusion mag exclusive for issue 002. these photos run with the feature built from skye's interview with imani brown, our lead journalist and executive editor. the pictures should feel like the same story skye tells imani.

skye making music, shot like an editorial. not a studio session and not bts. a girl with a little piano, a mic and her headphones, so locked in she forgot we were there.

the board is moodygirl. girls on the floor of their own rooms with the gear spread out around them. cables everywhere, keys, records, a laptop glowing, headphones half on. warm lamp light or a hard flash, a little grain, skin that looks real. the attitude is the whole thing: bored, cool, in her head. and when she finally looks at you, it hits.

we're shooting inside and outside the treehouse. inside, the little room is already halfway there: zebra print, pink, a big rug, skylights. outside, the rug goes down in the leaves, the piano sits on the deck, the mic stand goes in the dirt by the stairs. the treehouse is the free part, the gear and the styling are the moody part. it should feel like skye built her own studio up in the trees.

sun's down around 5:30. outside while we have light, then we move inside and the flash comes out for the night frames.

03 · moodygirl, in the trees

the aesthetic.

03

bedroom producer energy, in a treehouse. floors, rugs, cords and keys. somebody's room at 2am, tungsten warm, or a hard on-camera flash that flattens everything and makes it feel like a disposable. white tees and crew socks next to sheer black tights. treehouses, stairs, a lamp and a chair sitting in the middle of the woods. free and a little wild, but she's still the coolest person here.

board pulls for mood. we're borrowing the feeling, not copying anybody's shot.

in her zoneeyes on the keys, the mic or nowhere. she looks in the lens maybe once, and it counts.
gear in framepiano, mic, headphones or a cable in every shot. the music is the story.
inside + outthe room for the floor and daybed frames. the stairs, deck and yard for the free ones.
warm, then flashgolden light while it lasts. after 5:30 it's direct flash and the string lights.

04 · location

the treehouse.

04

the atlanta treehouse escape, east point. a treehouse on 2.4 wooded acres. we're shooting inside and out. outside: the stairs around the trunk, the deck, the yard and the string lights at night. inside: one small room, loud with color. zebra print, pink, sage walls, a big rug and a daybed.

location photos are from the peerspace listing.

06 · wardrobe

what she wears.

06

moodygirl, first and always. dark, a little undone, a little dangerous. little tops, sheer black tights, a lot of leg, hair doing whatever it wants. sexy but not trying, like she got dressed for herself and the session ran late. the cozy pieces only show up to make the moody ones hit harder.

moodygirlthe base of everything. black bralette or a little knit top, sheer black tights, pointed heels or a long low skirt.
the session fitoversized white tee or a baby tee, white crew socks, low sweats. thrown over the moody piece, never instead of it.
a printleopard like the unimpressed cover, polka dot or zebra. zebra plays right off the wall inside the treehouse.
face + hairsmudgy eye, real skin, hair big and wild or slicked back. the face does half the moody work.
bring layers.

these refs are for the feel, not exact pieces. bring a few options and we'll pick on the day. it's december and it gets cold fast once the sun's gone, so bring a coat or a blanket for between shots.
